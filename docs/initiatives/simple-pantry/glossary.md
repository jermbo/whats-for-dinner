# Glossary: a simple pantry

The [decision log](decision-log.md) uses words that the [glossary](../../glossary.md) of the wiki does not have, and it changes the meaning of some words. This page gives each of them one meaning. The page is temporary: a word goes to the wiki glossary when its decision is made.

## The words of a package

| Word          | Meaning                                                                                                       |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Food item     | A kind of food, such as milk or eggs. A recipe finds a food item. It is one note.                             |
| Package       | One container of a food item that you can hold in your hand: one jug of milk, one carton of eggs.            |
| Package size  | The amount of a package when it is new: 1 gal, or 12 eggs. A number and a unit from the unit list, or a count. |
| Counted       | A food item that you count, such as eggs. Its package has a count left: 4 of 12.                              |
| Measured      | A food item that you measure, such as milk or rice. Its package has a level left.                             |
| Unit list     | The fixed list of units that a package size can have. You select a unit, and you do not type it.              |
| Open package  | A package that is not full: a jug at 1/4, or a carton with 4 eggs.                                            |
| Place         | Where a package is, such as "Fridge". The names come from a list in the settings.                             |

## The words of a screen

| Word          | Meaning                                                                                                          |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pantry bar    | One bar for a food item. It has one part for each package, as long as its package size.                          |
| Card          | The open line of a food item: its total, its pantry bar with an olive handle that you drag, and its buttons.     |
| Handle        | The olive mark on the package that you drag. It snaps to a quarter, or to one egg.                               |
| Focus         | The place that you look at. Its packages are dark on the pantry bar, and the packages of other places are grey.  |
| Audit         | The weekly walk through each place, in which you confirm or correct each package.                                |
| To check      | A food item that a meal could not take food from. The audit shows it first.                                     |
| Verdict       | What you think of a brand: "Buy again" or "Avoid". It shows on the photo of the brand in the store.              |

## Words that change

| Word  | Meaning today                                                            | Meaning in this initiative                                              |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Level | How much of a food item you have, against its full amount.               | What is left in one measured package: full, 3/4, 1/2, 1/4, or empty.    |
| Low   | All packages together are at or below the low line of the food item.     | Only one package is left, and it is at 1/4 or less.                     |
| Out   | Each row of the food item is an empty row, or the note has no row.       | The note has no package.                                                |
