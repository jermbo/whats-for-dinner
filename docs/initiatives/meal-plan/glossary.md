# Glossary: meal plan

This page had the words of the [decision log](decision-log.md) that were new, or that had a different meaning in the wiki. A word goes to the wiki glossary when its work becomes architecture.

## Each word is in the wiki glossary now

The [glossary](../../glossary.md) of the wiki has the words of this work: "meal", "dish", "main dish", "side", and "week". "Meal" changed from one recipe to a main dish and its sides. "Week" changed from the meals that you did not cook to the options that you planned.
