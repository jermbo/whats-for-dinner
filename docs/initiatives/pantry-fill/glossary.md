# Glossary: pantry fill

This page has the words of the [decision log](decision-log.md) that are new. A word goes to the [wiki glossary](../../glossary.md) when its work becomes architecture.

## The words

| Word       | Meaning                                                                                                              |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Scan       | The camera reads the barcode of a package. The phone does it, with no connection.                                    |
| Lookup     | One request to Open Food Facts with the barcode number. It gives the product.                                        |
| Product    | What Open Food Facts knows of a barcode: a name such as "Bush's Best Black Beans", the brands, the quantity, and a photo. |
| Barcode of a brand | The number of the barcode, on the line of a brand in the food item note. A second scan finds the brand with no lookup. |
| Count      | How many packages of one product you put on the shelf at one time.                                                   |
