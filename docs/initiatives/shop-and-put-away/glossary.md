# Glossary: Shop and Put away

This page has the words of the [decision log](decision-log.md) that are new, or that have a different meaning in the wiki. A word goes to the [wiki glossary](../../glossary.md) when its work becomes architecture.

## The words

| Word         | Meaning                                                                                                   |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tick         | A tap on a row of the Shop: the check fills, the name is struck, and the item goes into the cart at once. |
| Flying name  | The name of an item on a small olive pill. It flies in an arc from a row to the cart, or from the cart back. |
| Shelf        | The button at the top left of the stage Shop, with the count of the items to get. It opens the list.       |
| Cart view    | The dark view of the items in the cart. It grows from the cart button, at the top right, and fills the stage. |
| Undo bar     | The bar under your thumb for a few seconds after a tick: "✓ Eggs is in the cart · Undo".                  |
