# Glossary: holiday planner

The [decision log](decision-log.md) uses words that the [glossary](../../glossary.md) of the wiki does not have. This page gives each of them one meaning. The page is temporary: a word goes to the wiki glossary when its work becomes architecture.

## The words of an occasion

| Word             | Meaning                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Occasion         | One day on which you cook many dishes, often with other people: Thanksgiving, or "Sam's birthday". It has a name and a date.                  |
| Occasion note    | The one note of an occasion, in the occasions folder. It holds the date, each dish, and your tasks.                                           |
| Occasions folder | The folder of the occasion notes. It is a setting, and it starts as `Larder/Occasions`.                                                       |
| Active           | An occasion is active from the moment that its note exists, up to the day after its date. It is "Next up" or in "Upcoming" in the Occasions part.          |
| Dish             | One food of an occasion: a recipe, such as the roast turkey, or only a name, such as "Pumpkin pie". A meal of the week is not a dish.          |
| Course           | Where a dish goes in the menu: starter, main, side, dessert, drink, or a word of your own.                                                     |
| Person           | Who cooks or brings a dish, such as "Mum". A dish with no person is yours, and a dish with the person `?` is open.                           |
| Your dish        | A dish with no person. Only your dishes put food on the list, and only they have "Cook".                                                        |
| Scale            | How many times you make a dish for the occasion, such as `×3`. With no scale, it is `×1`.                                                      |
| Occasion task    | A task of your own below the heading `## To do` of the occasion note, such as "Borrow chairs". It can have a day.                              |
| Guest            | A person who comes to the occasion, on the guest list of the note. A guest with no job is a visitor.                                            |
| Party size       | How many people come with one line of the guest list, such as "The Smiths ×4". With no number, it is 1.                                        |
| Job              | A thing that a person brings: a dish, a course with no dish yet, such as "Dessert", or a thing that is not food, such as "Plates".             |
| Open job         | A job with the person `?`: nobody brings it yet. The screen shows "Who brings?".                                                              |

"Meal" keeps its meaning: a recipe in the weekly plan. So the plugin does not call a dish of an occasion a meal.
