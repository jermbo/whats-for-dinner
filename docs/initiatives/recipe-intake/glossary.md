# Glossary: recipe intake

The [decision log](decision-log.md) uses words that the [glossary](../../glossary.md) of the wiki does not have. This page gives each of them one meaning. The page is temporary: a word goes to the wiki glossary when its decision is made.

## The words of intake

| Word          | Meaning                                                                                                                 |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Intake        | The work that makes a recipe note from a source, such as a card, a cookbook, or a web page, up to "Save".               |
| Source photo  | A photo of the original of a recipe, such as the card or the page of the cookbook. It stays with the recipe.            |
| Step photo    | A photo that shows one step of a recipe, in the note below that step.                                                   |
| Stub          | A food item note that intake makes for an ingredient with no food item. It has no row, so the food item is out.         |
| Line          | One ingredient or one step in a field of intake. It is not a row of the pantry.                                        |
| Gallery       | The view of the records of a recipe that have a photo: each photo with its date, its rating, and its note.              |

The owner said "pantry item". The wiki word is "food item": one food of the pantry, as one note.
