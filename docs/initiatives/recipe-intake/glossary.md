# Glossary: recipe intake

The [decision log](decision-log.md) uses words that the [glossary](../../glossary.md) of the wiki does not have. This page gives each of them one meaning. The page is temporary: a word goes to the wiki glossary when its decision is made.

## The words of intake

Each word of this initiative is in the wiki glossary now: intake, line, stub, source photo, step photo, and gallery.

The owner said "pantry item". The wiki word is "food item": one food of the pantry, as one note.
