# Glossary: style guide

The [decision log](decision-log.md) uses words that the [glossary](../../glossary.md) of the wiki does not have. This page gives each of them one meaning. The page is temporary: a word goes to the wiki glossary when its decision is made.

## The words of the look

These words of this initiative are in the wiki [glossary](../../glossary.md) now: look, token, scale, palette token, role token, piece token, piece, frame, and style guide.

The word "step" for one value of a scale did not go. The wiki glossary has "step" for one instruction of a recipe, and one word has one meaning. The style guide says "a value of the scale".
