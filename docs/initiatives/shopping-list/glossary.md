# Glossary: shopping list

The [decision log](decision-log.md) uses words that the [glossary](../../glossary.md) of the wiki does not have, or has with a different meaning. This page gives each of them one meaning. The page is temporary: a word goes to the wiki glossary when the work becomes architecture.

## The words of the list

| Word          | Meaning                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| List          | The items to buy at the next trip. The plugin calculates it, and the Shop part shows it. "Shopping list" and "grocery list" are two more names for it. |
| Trip          | One visit to a store. It can be on any day. It is open from your first action until put away ends it, and one trip is open at a time.                  |
| Choice        | An item that is on the list because you put it there: a suggestion that you accepted, or an item of your own.                                          |
| Shopping note | The note of one trip. It starts with your first action for that trip. You do not shop from it.                                                         |
| Receipt       | The shopping note of a trip that is complete: each item that you bought, with its price. Its name is the day of the trip.                              |
| Staple        | A food item with a label that tells that you always want this food, such as bread or salt.                                                             |
| Kind          | How often you buy a staple: "Often", "Sometimes", or "Seldom". You select it when you label the food.                                                  |
| Often         | The kind of a staple that is a suggestion at each trip, such as bread.                                                                                 |
| Sometimes     | The kind of a staple that the plugin asks about 3 weeks after its purchase, such as cereal.                                                            |
| Seldom        | The kind of a staple that the plugin asks about 2 months after its purchase, such as salt.                                                             |
| Suggestion    | A food item that needs a look, with the reason. You accept it or you skip it. It is a row of the list only after you accept it.                        |
| Purchase date | The day on which you put a food item away the last time. It is a property of the food item note.                                                       |
