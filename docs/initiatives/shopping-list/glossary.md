# Glossary: shopping list

This page had the words of the [decision log](decision-log.md) that were new, or that had a different meaning in the wiki. A word goes to the wiki glossary when its work becomes architecture.

## Each word is in the wiki glossary now

The [glossary](../../glossary.md) of the wiki has the words of this work: "list", "trip", "shopping note", "choice", "suggestion", "receipt", "staple", "kind", and "purchase date". The meaning of "Often", "Sometimes", and "Seldom" is in the row "Kind".
