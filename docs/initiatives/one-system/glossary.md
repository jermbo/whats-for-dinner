# Glossary: one system

This page has the words of the [decision log](decision-log.md) that are new, or that have a different meaning in the wiki. A word goes to the [wiki glossary](../../glossary.md) when its work becomes architecture.

## The words

| Word     | Meaning                                                                                                                             |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Circle   | The shape of each change. It grows from the point of your tap until it covers the card or the screen, and it goes back to that point. |
| Ink      | The colour role `selected` as a circle that opens a card. The text of the card changes to `on-selected` when the circle covers about half. |
| Paper    | The colour role `surface` as a circle over an ink card. The edit form is on it.                                                      |
| Meter    | The bar of an open recipe, with one piece for each ingredient, in the colour of its state.                                           |
| Pill     | The main action of a card: round ends, the accent colour, and the arrow in a circle.                                                 |
| Step back | What the other cards do when a card is open: they get lighter.                                                                      |
