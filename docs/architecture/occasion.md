# Occasion

For Thanksgiving you cook many dishes, Mum brings the pie, and the Smiths only come. The weekly [Plan](plan.md) holds meals, and it cannot tell who brings what. So an occasion is a plan of its own, for one day. This page tells what an occasion is, how the kitchen shows it, and what it puts on the list.

## One note, a name, and a day

An occasion is one [occasion note](occasion-note.md) in the occasions folder of the [settings](settings.md). The name of the note is the name of the occasion, such as "Thanksgiving 2026", and its property `date` is the day. Each dish is one task line: a recipe or only a name, with its course, its scale, and its person. The note also has the guest list and the tasks of the day.

## The Occasions part

The kitchen has a fifth part, Occasions, at all times. From the top it has "New occasion", the ink card "Next up", one row of chips, and a short row for each other occasion. "Next up" is the nearest occasion that is not past, with four numbers: the people who come, the open jobs, the food to buy, and the tasks to do. The chips filter the rows ("Upcoming", "Past", "All") and set their sequence. A tap on an occasion opens its screen.

"New occasion", or the command of the same name, asks for a name and a day. It can copy a past occasion, so that next Thanksgiving starts from this one.

An occasion is active from the moment that its note exists, up to the day after its date. You never set a status: the note and the date tell it. An occasion is past when its date is before today. The note stays as the record of the day, and only "Delete occasion" on its screen moves it to the trash.

## Guests and jobs

The guest list holds each person who comes, with a party size, such as "The Smiths ×4". "N coming" is you and each party size. A job is a thing that a person brings: a dish, a course with no dish yet, such as "Dessert", or a thing that is not food, such as "Plates", with the course `things`. A guest with no job is a visitor. A job that nobody brings yet has the person `?`: it is open. Remove a guest, and each job of that guest becomes open.

## The screen of an occasion

The screen is one page. The ink card of "Next up" is its top, with "⋯" to open the note or to delete the occasion. A number of the card scrolls to its section.

- **Who brings** has a place card for you, for each guest with a job, and for the open jobs. Its second tab is the guest list: one short row for each guest. Most guests only come, so they have a row and no card.
- **The menu** has a card for each course: starter, main, side, dessert, drink, things, then your own words, such as "bread". An empty course is a dashed card, as a slot of a sign-up sheet. A dish with no course gets the [type](type.md) of its recipe.
- **A tap on a dish** opens "Who brings?", a sheet from the bottom with a large button for each person, the courses, the scales, and "Remove dish".

## Your dishes, and only yours

Only your dishes put food on the [list](shopping.md), because Mum buys her own pumpkins. An open job and a thing put nothing there. Your dishes go on the list 7 days before the date, so a plan in October does not put a turkey on each list for five weeks. Before that, "To buy" shows what is missing, and "Add to the list" puts one row on the list early.

On the day, "Cook" opens a dish of yours in [Cook Mode](cook-mode.md), with its scale at the top. Each dish has a check: a dish is ready when you cooked it, or when it came.
