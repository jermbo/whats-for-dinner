# Amount bar

The pantry is only useful when it is correct. If a correction needs the keyboard, you do not make it. This page tells how the bar of an open row lets you set an amount with one drag.

## You look at the bag, not at a number

You stand at the shelf with the bag of rice in your hand. You do not know that it has 1.25 kg. You see that it is a quarter full. So the bar asks what you see: you drag it to a quarter.

## The bar of an open row

A tap on a row opens it. The length of the bar is the full amount of the food item, as in its [level](level.md). The bar has one piece for each place. Rice has two pieces: the kitchen pantry and the downstairs pantry.

You update one place at a time. It starts as the place of the row that you opened, and you can select a different one. Its piece is the last one, next to the green handle. The pieces of the other places do not move when you drag.

A row with no number, such as "a full bag", has no bar. It opens as a form.

## The amount snaps to round steps

The bar has about 20 steps from empty to full, and each step is a round amount. For rice with a full amount of 5 kg, a step is 250 g. For 12 eggs, a step is 1 egg. A step of a count is never less than a half.

The bar stops when all places together have the full amount. For more than that, use the form.

## The buttons of the bar

| Button                      | What it does                                                    |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Done"                      | Writes each place that you changed into the note.               |
| "Used up"                   | Sets the place to nothing. Its row goes out of the table.       |
| "Place, date, and low line" | Opens the form of that row, for all that the bar cannot change. |

When all places together are below the low line, the bar is amber while you drag.

You can also set the amount with the arrow keys.
