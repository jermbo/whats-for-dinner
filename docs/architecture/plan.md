# Plan

At 18:00, after a hard day, you cannot decide what to cook. So you decide at a calm moment, for the full week. This page tells how the plan holds those decisions, and why a meal has no day.

## A meal has no fixed day

A plan with a day for each meal breaks on the first bad day. So a meal is only "in the week". On the day itself, you tap "Today" on the meal that you will cook. Only a meal of today has the button "Cook". If the day goes wrong, "Not today" puts the meal back. The selection ends with the day.

## The week has room for each type

The type of a meal is the first tag of its [recipe](recipes.md): dinner, breakfast, lunch, or snack. The week has room for 7 meals of each type, one for each day. You do not have to fill it. A type of your own, such as "dessert", shows when it has a meal.

A meal that you cooked gives its place back, and it stays in view until you start a new week.

## The plugin proposes, and you select

Below the week, the plan shows three ideas. They are the recipes that are best for your pantry and that are not in the week. One tap adds an idea to the week.

## The meal card tells what is missing

Each meal shows its count, such as "7 of 9", and what that means for the week: "2 to buy", or "All in the pantry". When the plugin cannot [count](match.md) a line, such as 1 onion against 500 g, the card adds "1 to check". You see on Sunday what to look at, not at the stove.

## Preparation comes before the meal

Some meals need work before you can cook, such as beef that must come out of the freezer. A recipe tells this in a callout of the type "prep", its [preparation](preparation.md). The meal shows the preparation, with a check box. After you confirm it, "Cook" is the main button of the meal.

## The plan is one note

The plan is one note, the [plan note](plan-note.md). It has one task for each meal:

```markdown
- [ ] [[Recipes/Tacos|Tacos]] · 2026-10-11 · prep done
- [x] [[Recipes/Pancakes|Pancakes]]
```

A task that is done is a meal that you cooked. The date is the day for which you selected the meal. The words "prep done" tell that you confirmed the preparation.

The plugin changes only these lines. All other text of the note stays, so the note can also have your own text for the week.
