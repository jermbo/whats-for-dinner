# Low food

The shopping list comes from the plan. But some food you want always, plan or no plan: flour, rice, milk. This page tells how such food gets on the list.

## The plan does not see the flour

No meal of this week needs flour. So the list has no flour, and the flour gets less each week. In the week that the pancakes are in the plan, you have 200 g and it is Sunday morning. The list must see the flour before that week.

## One chip puts all low food on the list

The Shop part has the chip "Low food", with the number of food items that it adds. One tap puts them on the list, and one more tap takes them off. The plugin keeps your choice, so the chip stays on until you change it.

The chip adds two kinds of food item:

- **Low:** the food item is below its low line, with all places together. The [level](level.md) page tells how the plugin decides this.
- **Out:** the food item has no row.

A row of the list shows which kind it is, with the sticker "Low" or "Out".

## The amount makes the food item full again

The amount to buy is the full amount less what you have. Flour has 200 g of 1 kg, so the list says "Flour, 800 g". Milk is out and its full amount is 1 l, so the list says "Milk, 1 l".

A food item that is out and has no full amount is on the list with no amount. You decide how much you buy.

## A meal can need more

A food item can be low, and a meal can need it too. Then the list has one row, with the larger of the two amounts. The pancakes need 200 g of flour, and to fill the flour you buy 800 g. The row says 800 g, which covers the two.

## What is never low

A food item that the plugin cannot count has no level, so it is never low. Water with the row "tap" is an example. It gets on the list only when it is out.
