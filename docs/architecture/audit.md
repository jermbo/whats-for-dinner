# Audit

The pantry is close to the truth, but not exact. A level rounds, and a meal cannot count an onion against a bag. So once a week, before you plan, you walk the places and you check what is really there. This page tells how the audit keeps that walk short.

## You walk the places, one at a time

"Audit" in the head of the Pantry part starts the walk. It shows one place at a time, in the sequence of the places in the [settings](settings.md), and then each other place that has food. The head says "Fridge · 2 of 3".

Each food item of the place shows with its bar, with this place in focus. You look at the shelf, and for each food item you do one of two things:

- **"Right"**: the food is as the pantry says. The line goes grey, gets a check mark, and moves to the end of the place.
- **A tap**: the [card](card.md) of the food item opens. You drag, empty, or add a package, and "Done" writes it. The food item is confirmed too.

"Next place" goes on. After the last place, the button is "Done".

## The food to check comes first

A meal that could not count an ingredient writes `check: true` into its [food item note](food-item-note.md): 1 onion against a bag of 1 kg. Such a food item is first in its place, with the sticker "Check". "Right", or a change in its card, takes the mark away.

## You can stop, and continue later

An audit is a part of a session of 15 to 30 minutes, and a call can stop it. "Stop" leaves the audit. The data file keeps the last place that you finished, so the next "Audit" starts at the place after it. After the last place, the next audit starts at the first place again.

An example: you finish "Kitchen pantry" and "Fridge", and you stop. On Sunday, "Audit" starts at "Freezer", "3 of 3".
