# Brands

The shelf has four taco seasonings, and the list gives you a name. In the store, you look for a package, not for a word. This page tells how each brand gets a photo, and how the brand that you take goes into the history.

## A brand is a package that you can buy

Taco seasoning has Brand A, Brand B, and Brand C. A brand has a name, a photo of its package, or the two. In the store, with one hand, you take the photo and type nothing. So a brand with no name is complete: it shows only its photo, and you can give it a name later.

## The note keeps the brands

The brands are in the section "Brands" of the [food item note](food-item-note.md): one list item for each brand, with its photo on the line below it. In Obsidian, you see each brand with its photo with no plugin.

## A photo is a file of the pantry folder

The photo goes into the folder `Photos` of the pantry folder, such as `Larder/Pantry/Photos`. The plugin makes the folder with the first photo. The name tells where the photo belongs: `Taco seasoning - Brand A.jpg`. A brand with no name gives the date: `Taco seasoning - brand 2026-10-18.jpg`. A name in use gets a number, from 2: `Taco seasoning - Brand A 2.jpg`. The size is the size of a [recipe photo](end-of-a-meal.md).

The plugin renames no photo and deletes no photo. The link in the note is the connection, not the name of the file. So a new name of a brand does not break it, and a brand that goes leaves its photo in the folder.

## You can take a photo at each moment that shows the food

| Moment                                | What the photo makes                                    |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| The form of a food item in the Pantry | A brand that you can give a name and a new photo.       |
| "Add to pantry", also in setup        | The same, for a new food item.                          |
| The row of the stage Shop             | A brand with no name, shown large with "In cart".       |
| The open line of put away             | A brand with no name, selected for the line.            |

## The brand that you bought

In the [stage Shop](shopping.md), a tap on a photo shows it large, and "In cart" ticks the row with that brand. The [shopping note](shopping-note.md) keeps it after the link of the task. [Put away](put-away.md) starts from it, and the [receipt](receipt.md) keeps it with its own price.

A brand on a receipt is the link to its first photo: `[[Taco seasoning - Brand A.jpg]]`. A brand with no photo gives its name. So a receipt still finds Brand A after it gets a new name.
