# Planning preferences

A planning preference changes what the app proposes: how many meals, which food it doubts, and the sequence of the shopping list. It is a fact about your kitchen, so it is the same on each of your devices.

## The six preferences

| Preference                 | Default                      | What it changes                                                      |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Meals in a week            | 5                            | The number of slots that Today shows for the week.                   |
| First day of the week      | Monday                       | The day on which a week starts, for the meals and for the spending.  |
| Days before a doubt        | 7                            | The age at which a perishable food gets a doubt in the pantry check. |
| Sequence of the categories | Produce first, "Other" last  | The sequence of the groups on the shopping list.                     |
| Meal types                 | Breakfast, lunch, and dinner | The types of recipe that Menu proposes.                              |
| Servings of a new recipe   | 2                            | The number that the form of a new recipe starts with.                |

## The week is yours

Today shows the slots of the week that are open. A slot that you never fill is noise. With the correct number, an empty slot always means "plan one more meal".

The first day is the day of your usual trip to the store. If you shop on Saturday, your week starts on Saturday. Then a trip, and the meals that it pays for, are in the same week. This is important for your [spending by week](../shopping/spending-by-week.md).

## The list follows your store

The [shopping list](../shopping/in-the-store.md) shows its items in groups: produce, dairy, dry goods. You set the sequence of these groups to the sequence of the aisles in your store. Then you go through the store one time, from the top of the list to its end.

You drag a row to change the sequence.

## A doubt comes at the correct time

In the pantry check, the app asks you only about the food that it is not sure about. A perishable food with no new stock for seven days is such a food. If you shop two times in a week, seven days is too slow, and 4 is better.

## The app proposes only what you plan

If you plan only the dinner, a breakfast recipe is a card that you must refuse each time. With "Dinner" as the only meal type, Menu does not propose it. The recipe stays in Recipes.

## An example

Your store starts with the bakery, and you shop on Saturday. You drag "Bakery" to the top of the categories and set the first day to Saturday. The next list starts with the bread.
