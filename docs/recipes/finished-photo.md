# The finished photo and the cover

A list of recipes with photos of your own plates makes you want to cook. This page tells how those photos get into the app, and which one a recipe shows.

## The app asks at the end

The last card of Cook mode has the "Cooked" button, and the screen after "Cooked" has one large camera button, "Take a photo of your meal". The moment is short, because the plate is full and people wait. So the photo is one tap. The photo is optional: you can leave the screen with no photo.

Each [cook session](cook-session.md) has one finished photo or none.

## The cover

The cover is the photo that a recipe shows in each list and at the top of its page.

| The recipe has     | The cover is                                               |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| No finished photo  | A placeholder picture.                                     |
| One finished photo | That photo. The first one becomes the cover automatically. |
| Two or more        | The photo that you select. Until you select, the first.    |

A new photo never changes the cover. The reason: the newest photo is not always the best. A plate in bad light on a Tuesday must not replace the photo that you like.

## The carousel

When a recipe has two or more finished photos, the top of its page is a carousel: you swipe from one photo to the next. It opens at the cover, which has a mark. Each other photo has a "Set as cover" button.

The carousel shows finished photos only. A [step photo](step-photos.md) stays with its step.

## Finished photos have no limit

The app keeps each finished photo, because it is the memory of one evening. The photos are small, and [they add up slowly](photo-weight.md).

## An example

| Date       | What you do                  | The cover                       |
| ---------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 3 October  | You take photo A.            | A, because it is the first.     |
| 17 October | You take photo B.            | A. The page has a carousel now. |
| 18 October | You tap "Set as cover" on B. | B                               |
