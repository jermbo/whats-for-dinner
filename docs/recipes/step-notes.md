# Step notes

You learn something each time you cook: the curry needs less salt, the pan must be hotter. A week later you do not remember it. A step note keeps that thought on the step where you need it.

## One tap, on the step

Each step card in Cook mode has a note button. You type some words, or you speak them with the microphone of the keyboard: "Less salt. One spoon is sufficient."

## The next cook shows it

The next time you cook the recipe, the step card shows the note below the text, with its date: "3 October: Less salt. One spoon is sufficient." You read it at the moment when the salt is in your hand.

A step card shows each note of its step, the newest first. A tap on a note lets you change it or delete it.

## Why a note is not a change of the recipe

You could correct the text of the step. But at the stove you have no time to edit, and you are not sure yet. A note is the quick record of one evening. When two notes say the same thing, you correct the text in [Write mode](write-mode.md) and delete the notes.

## Where a note is stored

A note belongs to the [cook session](cook-session.md): the record of that one evening. It has its text and the ID of its step.

So the notes are also a history: each cook has its date, its rating, its photo, and its notes.

A note names its step by the ID, not by the number. If you put a new step before it, the note [stays with the correct step](data-model.md).

## An example

| Date       | Step 4 shows            | You write                   |
| ---------- | ----------------------- | --------------------------- |
| 3 October  | No note.                | "Less salt."                |
| 10 October | "3 October: Less salt." | Nothing. The curry is good. |
| 17 October | "3 October: Less salt." | "Add the cream at the end." |
