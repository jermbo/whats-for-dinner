# Features

The plugin makes your vault a kitchen that knows what it has. This page tells what you can do with it, and why you would.

## The pantry knows what you have

You record your food one time: a full bag of rice, 6 eggs. From then on, the plugin keeps the count. You do not look into the cupboard before you plan.

## The recipes that use your food come first

The recipe list sorts by your pantry, not by name. Each recipe shows how much of it you have: "7 of 9 in the pantry". Food that spoils soon moves its recipes up, so you use the spinach before it is waste.

## The week has a plan before it starts

At a calm moment, you choose the meals of the week. The plugin proposes recipes that use up your pantry. A meal has no fixed day, so one bad day does not break the plan.

## The shopping list has only the missing items

The list is the plan minus the pantry. If two meals need onions, the list has one row with the total. In the store, one tap marks an item. At home, one action puts the full trip into the pantry.

## Cook Mode shows one step at a time

The screen shows one step in large type, with the ingredients of that step. The buttons are large, because your hands are dirty. The screen stays on, and a step with a time has its timer. When you finish, the plugin takes the food out of the pantry. You can add a photo, a rating, and a note for the next time.

## Your recipes are notes that last

A recipe is a plain note: a title, the ingredients, and the steps. You type it, or you copy it from a cookbook or a family card. Any text editor can open the file, with or without the plugin.

## Your phone and your computer, with no account

The plugin works on the two devices and with no connection. Obsidian moves the vault between them. The plugin sends your data to no server.
