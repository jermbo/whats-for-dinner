# Personas

The plugin is for one cook. But that cook is not the same person at the stove and at the desk. This page gives the five moments of the cook. Each user story names one of them, so that the story is built for that moment.

## One person, five moments

| Persona | The moment                                              | What makes it hard                                        |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Planner | A calm moment of the week, on the phone or the computer | To decide for 7 days with no look into the cupboard.      |
| Shopper | In the store, with the phone in one hand                | No time, no keyboard, and sometimes no connection.        |
| Keeper  | At the shelf at home, with food to count or to put away | Each entry is work. If it is not one tap, it is not made. |
| Cook    | At the stove, with dirty hands and eyes on the pan      | To read small text, and to touch the screen.              |
| Writer  | At the computer, in a note of the vault                 | To keep recipes in a form that lasts, with no plugin.     |

## Why a story names a persona

"The user can change an amount" tells nothing about how. "The keeper can change an amount" tells much: the keeper stands at a shelf with a bag in one hand. So the change must be one drag, and not a number that you type.

The persona is the first test of a design. If the design asks the shopper to type, or the cook to read a paragraph, the design is wrong.

## What the personas share

All five use the same vault, mostly on a phone, and often with no connection. None of them has an account. These are not limits of one moment. They are the rules of the [vision](vision.md), and each story follows them.
