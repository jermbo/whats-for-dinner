# Vision

This page is the true north of the plugin. If a feature idea conflicts with this page, the idea changes or this page changes.

## The vision statement

A plugin for Obsidian that knows what is in your pantry, so that you plan, shop, and cook with the food that you have.

## The problem

Three things go wrong when the kitchen has no plan.

- **At 18:00.** You come home after a hard day. You look at the pantry and have no idea what to cook. The chicken is still in the freezer. You order pizza.
- **At the store.** You go with no list. You buy items that are already in the pantry, and you forget the one item that a recipe needs.
- **In the recipe box.** Your recipes are in written notes, in cookbooks, and on old family cards. They are not in one place, and an app that closes can take them with it.

The results are wasted food, more money, more time, and the same five meals.

## The result that the plugin must give

- You know what you have, with no look into the cupboard.
- You choose the meals of the week at a calm moment, from recipes that use up what you have.
- You go to the store with a list of the missing items only.
- You cook one step at a time, with dirty hands, and the pantry is correct when you finish.
- Your recipes are plain notes that you can read in 20 years.

## The pantry is the center

A recipe viewer starts at the recipe. This plugin starts at the food that you have. The plan, the shopping list, and the sequence of the recipes all come from the pantry.

## Why Obsidian

The tool must be on your phone and on your computer, and the data must stay yours.

- Your recipes are notes in a vault. The plugin reads them where they are.
- A vault is a folder of files. You can read a recipe or a photo with no plugin.
- Obsidian moves the vault between your devices. The plugin needs no server and no account.
- All functions work with no connection, in the kitchen and in the store.

## What the plugin is not

It is not a service, and it is not for a group. It is for one cook, on the devices of that cook.
