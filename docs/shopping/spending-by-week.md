# Spending by week

You want to know how much you spend on food, from one week to the next. The trips screen answers this question with one line for each week.

## The problem

A list of [trips](prices-and-trips.md) does not tell you the week. One week has one large trip. The next week has a large trip, and two small ones for the food that you forgot. To compare the two weeks, you must add the numbers in your head.

## One line for each week

The trips screen puts the trips in groups, one group for each week. The newest week is first. Each group starts with the sum of its trips.

> This week: 61.30, 2 trips
> Last week: 48.75, 1 trip, and 3 items with no price

You read down the lines and see the direction: more, less, or the same.

Below the line of a week are its trips. A tap on a trip opens its purchases, each with its price.

## When a week starts

A week starts on the first day of your week, which is a [planning preference](../settings/planning-preferences.md). A trip belongs to the week in which it started.

If you shop on Saturday, set the first day to Saturday. Then the large trip, and the meals that it pays for, are in the same week.

## The sum does not hide a gap

A price is optional, so a purchase can have none. The sum of a week has only the purchases with a price. The line tells how many purchases have none, in the same way as the cost of one trip. So you know when a week looks cheap only because prices are missing.

## Where the screen is

The Shop screen has a "Trips" link, because the question "what did I spend?" comes when you look at the list. The end of ["Put away"](put-away.md) has the same link, next to the cost of the trip that you completed.

## What the screen does not do

It has no budget and no chart. It also does not know the store of a trip. The numbers by week are the first step, and they need no new input from you.

## An example

Your week starts on Saturday. On Saturday 3 October you spend 42.80. On Tuesday you get limes and cream for 6.10. On Saturday 10 October a new week starts.

> Week of 3 October: 48.90, 2 trips
