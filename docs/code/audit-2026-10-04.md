# Code audit of 4 October 2026

This page is a record, not a rule. It tells how the code compared with [the rules of this topic](README.md) on one day: 208 files and 20,446 lines in `src`. The [cleanup plan](cleanup-plan.md) comes from it.

## The grade: B minus

The base is strong, and the screens are not. The database functions, the types, and the words are of a high standard. The files that the owner sees most, the large components and the screens, do too many jobs.

## The eight large files

`StepFields` (741 lines), `MealHand` (579), `CookMode` (486), `PutAwayCard` (388), `MenuCard` (387), `MealSheet` (381), `MealCardBack` (324), and the Today screen (303).

`MealHand` keeps the order of the pile, has five motions, stores a hint, and speaks to screen readers. `PutAwayCard` is two cards in one: a flag named `amending` is in each of its functions.

## The six rules that are in more than one place

1. The food to use first: the same nine lines on Today and on Menu.
2. What the menu needs: the same call with four arguments, in four files.
3. The cart: three definitions, with two different sort orders.
4. The time of now: a shared clock, a private timer on Today, and a time that Menu reads one time only.
5. Open and close of a dialog: nine components.
6. A card that is a sheet on a phone and a panel on a desktop: two components.

## How the numbers were made

The grade is one reading of all files of `src`, plus counts from `grep`, `svelte-check`, and ESLint. It is not a measurement of how the app works for the owner.
