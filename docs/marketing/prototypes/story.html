Why it's different

Everything is connected to one thing: your pantry.

A recipe app starts at the recipe. This starts at the food you actually have. The plan, the list, and the cooking all flow from it — and back into it.

01

Pantry → Recipes

Recipes sort by what you have. Each shows "7 of 9 in the pantry," and food about to turn floats to the top.

02

Plan → Shopping list

The list is your plan minus your pantry. You buy only the gaps, plus staples when they run low.

03

Shop → Pantry

Back home, the cart becomes pantry rows and a receipt. Check the lines, tap put away, done.

04

Cook → Pantry

Finish a meal and the ingredients come out of the pantry on their own. It's correct when you plate.

The pantry

It knows every package, in every place.

One note per food, one row per package — tracked per place: kitchen, fridge, freezer, downstairs. One bar shows how much is left, and turns amber when you're low. Drag it to what you see: half a jug, four eggs.

Your kitchen, your notes

It's just Markdown. Yours in 20 years.

Every recipe, every food, every receipt is a plain note in your vault. Works offline, stays on your devices, no account, no telemetry. Recipes follow the open RecipeMD standard — with step photos, ratings, and a "more garlic next time" history on top.

✦ To remove it all, delete one folder. Your recipes stay.
# Chicken curry

A mild curry with coconut milk.

_dinner, chicken, one pot_
**4 servings**
---
- _500 g_ chicken thighs
- _1 can_ coconut milk
- salt
---
1. Cook the chicken for 5 minutes.
2. Add the coconut milk. Cook 15 min.
