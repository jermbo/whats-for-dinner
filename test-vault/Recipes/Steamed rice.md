# Steamed rice

Plain rice for a curry or a stir-fry.

*side, vegan*

**4 servings**

---

- *300 g* rice
- *450 ml* water
- *1/2 teaspoon* salt

---

1. Wash the rice until the water is clear.
2. Put the rice, the water, and the salt in a pot. Bring to a boil.
3. Put the lid on. Cook with low heat for 12 minutes.
4. Remove from the heat. Let it rest for 10 minutes with the lid on.
