# Garlic butter spaghetti

This note links its ingredients to the pantry notes.

*dinner, vegetarian, fast*

**2 servings**

---

- *200 g* [[Larder/Pantry/Spaghetti|spaghetti]]
- *40 g* [[Larder/Pantry/Butter|butter]]
- *4 cloves* [[Larder/Pantry/Garlic|garlic]], sliced
- *30 g* [[Larder/Pantry/Parmesan|parmesan]], grated
- [[Larder/Pantry/Black pepper|black pepper]]

---

1. Cook the spaghetti in salted water for 9 minutes. Keep 1 cup of the water.
2. Melt the butter in a pan with low heat. Cook the garlic for 2 minutes. Do not let it go brown.
3. Add the spaghetti and some of the water. Mix until the sauce is smooth.
4. Put the parmesan and the pepper on top.
