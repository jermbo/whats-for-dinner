# Overnight oats

Make it in the evening. The yogurt is not in the pantry.

*breakfast, fast*

**1 serving**

---

- *50 g* rolled oats
- *120 ml* milk
- *60 g* yogurt
- *1 tsp* honey
- *1* apple, grated

---

1. Mix the oats, the milk, the yogurt, and the honey in a jar.
2. Put the jar in the fridge for one night.
3. Add the apple in the morning.
