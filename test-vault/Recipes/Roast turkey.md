# Roast turkey

*main, holiday*

**12 servings**

---

- *5 kg* whole turkey
- *100 g* butter
- *2* onions
- *4* cloves garlic
- *1 tbsp* salt
- *1 tbsp* black pepper

---

> [!prep] Take the turkey out of the freezer 3 days before.

1. Heat the oven to 180 °C. Rub the turkey with the butter, the salt, and the pepper. Put the onions and the garlic inside.
2. Roast the turkey for 3 hours. Add a little water to the pan if it gets dry.
3. Check the thickest part of the leg. It must be 75 °C. If not, roast for 20 minutes more.
4. Cover the turkey with foil and let it rest for 30 minutes. Keep the juice for the gravy.
