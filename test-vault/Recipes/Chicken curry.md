# Chicken curry

A mild curry with coconut milk. It is good for a day when you have little time.

*dinner, chicken, one pot*

**4 servings**

---

- *500 g* chicken thighs
- *300 g* rice
- *1 can* coconut milk
- *1* onion
- *2 cloves* garlic
- *20 g* ginger
- *2 tablespoons* curry paste
- *1 tablespoon* oil
- *1* lime

---

1. Cook the rice as the bag tells you.
2. Cut the onion, the garlic, and the ginger into small pieces.
3. Heat the oil in a large pan. Cook the onion for 5 minutes.
4. Add the garlic, the ginger, and the curry paste. Cook for 1 minute.
5. Cut the chicken thighs into pieces and add them. Cook for 5 minutes.
6. Add the coconut milk. Cook with low heat for 15 minutes.
7. Add the juice of the lime. Serve with the rice.
