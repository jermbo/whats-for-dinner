# Thai green curry

Most of this recipe is not in the pantry.

*dinner, thai*

**4 servings**

---

- *400 ml* coconut milk
- *2 tbsp* green curry paste
- *400 g* chicken thighs, sliced
- *1 tbsp* fish sauce
- *1 handful* thai basil
- *300 g* rice

---

1. Cook the rice.
2. Heat a quarter of the coconut milk in a pan. Cook the curry paste in it for 2 minutes.
3. Add the chicken. Cook for 5 minutes.
4. Add the rest of the coconut milk and the fish sauce. Cook with low heat for 10 minutes.
5. Add the basil. Serve with the rice.
