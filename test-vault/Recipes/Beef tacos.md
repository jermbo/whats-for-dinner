# Beef tacos

The beef in the fridge is past its date, so the freezer pack is the one to use. There are fewer tortillas than the recipe needs.

*dinner, mexican*

**4 servings, 8 tacos**

---

## Filling

- *450 g* ground beef
- *28 g* taco seasoning
- *1* onion, chopped
- *1 tbsp* olive oil

## To serve

- *8* tortillas
- *100 g* cheddar cheese, grated
- *1 head* lettuce, shredded
- *120 ml* sour cream

---

1. Heat the oil in a pan with medium heat. Cook the onion for 3 minutes.
2. Add the beef. Cook for 8 minutes, and break it up with a spoon.
3. Add the taco seasoning and 150 ml of water. Cook for 5 minutes, until the sauce is thick.
4. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan for 30 seconds on each side.
5. Fill each tortilla with beef, cheese, lettuce, and sour cream.
