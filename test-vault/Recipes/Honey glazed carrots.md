# Honey glazed carrots

*side, vegetarian, fast*

**4 servings**

---

- *500 g* carrots
- *2 tbsp* honey
- *20 g* butter
- salt

---

1. Cut the carrots into sticks.
2. Cook them in salted water for 6 minutes. Drain.
3. Melt the butter and the honey in the pot. Add the carrots. Cook for 3 minutes, and stir until they shine.
