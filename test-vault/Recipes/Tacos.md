# Tacos

Tacos with ground beef. Each brand of taco seasoning is good.

*dinner, fast*

**4 servings**

---

- *500 g* ground beef
- *1 bag* taco seasoning
- *8* taco shells
- *1* onion
- *100 g* cheese
- *1* lime

---

1. Cut the onion. Cook it in a pan for 3 minutes.
2. Add the ground beef. Cook for 8 minutes, until it is brown.
3. Add the taco seasoning and a small glass of water. Cook for 5 minutes.
4. Heat the taco shells in the oven for 3 minutes.
5. Fill the taco shells. Put the cheese on top, and add the juice of the lime.
