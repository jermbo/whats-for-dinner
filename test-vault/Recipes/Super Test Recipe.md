# Super Test Recipe

_breakfast_

**4 servings**

---

- _200 g_ [[Larder/Pantry/Flour|flour]]
- _2_ [[Larder/Pantry/Eggs|eggs]]
- _1/4 cup_ [[Larder/Pantry/Butter|butter]]
- _1/2 cup_ [[Larder/Pantry/Milk|milk]]

---

1. Mix flour and milk for 1 minute
2. Mix eggs into bowl for 15 seconds
3. Heat a pan with some of the butter
4. Pour on batter and cook on one side for 1 minute or until bubbles
5. Flip for one more minute
6. Serve
