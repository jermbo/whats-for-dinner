# Chicken fried rice

Uses the chicken thighs in the fridge before their date. Cold rice from yesterday fries best.

*dinner, fast*

**4 servings**

---

- *300 g* rice
- *300 g* chicken thighs, cut small
- *2* eggs, beaten
- *150 g* frozen peas
- *2* carrots, diced
- *2 cloves* garlic
- *3 tbsp* soy sauce
- *2 tbsp* vegetable oil

---

1. Cook the rice. Spread it on a plate to cool.
2. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a large pan with high heat. Cook the chicken for 6 minutes. Put it on a plate.
3. Add the rest of the oil. Cook the carrots and the garlic for 3 minutes.
4. Push the vegetables to the side. Cook the eggs in the free part of the pan, and stir them.
5. Add the rice, the peas, and the chicken. Fry for 4 minutes.
6. Add the soy sauce and mix.
