# Guacamole

A fast dip for chips or tacos.

*dip, vegan, no cook*

**4 servings**

---

- *2* avocados
- *1* lime
- *1/2* onion
- *1/4 teaspoon* salt
- fresh coriander

---

1. Cut the avocados in half and remove the stones. Put the flesh in a bowl.
2. Mash the flesh with a fork.
3. Cut the onion into small pieces and add it.
4. Add the juice of the lime and the salt. Mix.
5. Put coriander leaves on top.
