# Pancakes

Thick pancakes for a slow morning.

*breakfast, vegetarian*

**8 pancakes**

---

## Dry

- *200 g* flour
- *2 teaspoons* baking powder
- *1 tablespoon* sugar
- *1 pinch* salt
- *200 g* flour
- *2 teaspoons* baking powder
- *1 tablespoon* sugar
- *1 pinch* salt
- *200 g* flour
- *2 teaspoons* baking powder
- *1 tablespoon* sugar
- *1 pinch* salt

## Wet

- *300 ml* milk
- *1* egg
- *30 g* butter
- *300 ml* milk
- *1* egg
- *30 g* butter

---

1. Mix the dry ingredients in a large bowl.
2. Wait for 30 seconds.
3. Melt the butter. Mix it with the milk and the egg.
4. Add the wet mix to the dry mix. Stir until there are no large lumps.
5. Let the batter rest for 10 minutes.
6. Heat a pan with medium heat. Cook each pancake for 2 minutes on each side.
