# Apple crumble

The recipe needs 4 apples, and the fridge has 2.

*dessert, baking, vegetarian*

**6 servings**

---

## Filling

- *4* apples, sliced
- *1 tbsp* sugar
- *1 tsp* cinnamon

## Topping

- *100 g* flour
- *75 g* butter, cold
- *75 g* brown sugar
- *50 g* rolled oats

---

1. Heat the oven to 190 °C.
2. Mix the apples with the sugar and the cinnamon. Put them in a baking dish.
3. Rub the butter into the flour with your fingers. Mix in the brown sugar and the oats.
4. Put the topping on the apples. Bake for 35 minutes, until it is brown.
