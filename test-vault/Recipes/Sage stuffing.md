# Sage stuffing

*side, holiday*

**10 servings**

---

- *600 g* white bread
- *80 g* butter
- *2* onions
- *3* stalks celery
- *2 tbsp* dried sage
- *500 ml* chicken stock

---

1. Cut the bread into cubes. Dry them in the oven at 150 °C for 20 minutes.
2. Melt the butter in a pan. Cook the onions and the celery for 8 minutes.
3. Mix the bread, the onions, the celery, and the sage. Pour in the stock.
4. Put it in a dish and bake at 180 °C for 40 minutes, until the top is brown.
