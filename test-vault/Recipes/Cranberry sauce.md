# Cranberry sauce

*side, holiday, make ahead*

**10 servings**

---

- *400 g* cranberries
- *200 g* sugar
- *150 ml* orange juice

---

1. Put the cranberries, the sugar, and the orange juice in a pot.
2. Cook on medium heat for 12 minutes, until the berries burst. Stir often.
3. Let it cool for 30 minutes. It gets thick as it cools. Keep it in the fridge.
