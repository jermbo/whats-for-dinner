# Banana oat muffins

The bananas are past their date, which is right for this recipe.

*baking, breakfast*

**12 muffins**

---

## Dry

- *150 g* rolled oats
- *120 g* flour
- *75 g* brown sugar
- *1 tsp* baking soda
- *1 tsp* cinnamon
- *1 pinch* salt

## Wet

- *3* bananas, mashed
- *1* egg
- *60 g* butter, melted
- *120 ml* milk
- *1 tsp* vanilla extract

---

1. Heat the oven to 180 °C. Put paper cups in a muffin pan.
2. Mix the dry ingredients in a large bowl.
3. Mix the wet ingredients in a second bowl.
4. Add the wet mix to the dry mix. Stir only until there is no dry flour.
5. Fill each cup to 3/4. Bake for 20 minutes.
