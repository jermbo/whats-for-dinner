# Spinach omelette

A fast meal that uses up spinach before it spoils.

*lunch, vegetarian, fast*

**1 serving**

---

- *3* eggs
- *100 g* spinach
- *30 g* cheese
- *1 teaspoon* butter
- salt
- pepper

---

1. Beat the eggs with salt and pepper.
2. Melt the butter in a pan. Cook the spinach for 2 minutes, until it is soft.
3. Add the eggs. Cook with low heat for 3 minutes.
4. Put the cheese on one half. Fold the omelette and serve.
