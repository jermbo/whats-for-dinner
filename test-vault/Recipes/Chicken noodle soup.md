# Chicken noodle soup

The broth and the elbow pasta are low, and the celery is missing.

*dinner, soup*

**6 servings**

---

- *450 g* chicken thighs
- *2 l* chicken broth
- *2* carrots, sliced
- *1* onion, diced
- *2 stalks* celery, sliced
- *150 g* elbow pasta
- *1 tbsp* olive oil
- salt
- black pepper

---

1. Heat the oil in a large pot. Cook the onion, the carrots, and the celery for 5 minutes.
2. Add the broth and the chicken. Bring to a boil, then cook with low heat for 20 minutes.
3. Take out the chicken. Pull it into small pieces with two forks.
4. Add the pasta to the pot. Cook for 8 minutes.
5. Put the chicken back. Add salt and pepper.
