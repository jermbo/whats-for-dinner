# Mashed potatoes

*side, vegetarian*

**4 servings**

---

- *1 kg* potatoes, peeled
- *60 g* butter
- *120 ml* milk, warm
- salt
- black pepper

---

1. Cut the potatoes into equal pieces. Put them in a pot of cold, salted water.
2. Bring to a boil. Cook for 15 minutes, until a knife goes in easily.
3. Drain the potatoes. Mash them with the butter.
4. Stir in the milk. Add salt and pepper.
