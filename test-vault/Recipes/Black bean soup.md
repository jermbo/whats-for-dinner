# Black bean soup

A pantry soup. The broth is in two places, and the cumin is not in the pantry.

*dinner, vegetarian*

**4 servings**

---

- *850 g* black beans (2 cans), drained
- *1 l* chicken broth
- *1* onion, diced
- *3 cloves* garlic
- *1 tsp* cumin
- *2 tbsp* olive oil
- salt, to taste

---

1. Heat the oil in a pot with medium heat. Cook the onion for 5 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and the cumin. Cook for 1 minute.
3. Add the beans and the broth. Cook with low heat for 20 minutes.
4. Mash half of the beans in the pot. Add salt.
