# Check list: the kitchen layout

This list tests the two stories of version 0.7.0: each place of the pantry is a bin, and the top of each part stays in view. Each line has one action and what you must see. The text in brackets is the story and its criterion.

## Before you start

- [ ] The build made `main.js` again. Put the plugin off and on, or restart Obsidian.
- [ ] The pantry is the sample set of 2026-10-19. The date of today matters for "past" and "soon": these checks use 2026-10-19.

## A. Each place is a bin

- [ ] On the desktop, make the kitchen tab about 1000 px wide, and open Pantry "By place". There are two columns of cards. "Kitchen pantry" is in one column, and "Fridge", "Freezer", and "Out" are in the other, with no empty space between them. (Story 1, AC 1)
- [ ] The head of "Fridge" shows "Fridge", "14", a tomato "1 past", an outline "4 soon", and an amber "2 low". (Story 1, AC 2)
- [ ] "Out" has a dashed edge and no card. Heavy cream and Shell pasta are dashed tags. (Story 1, requirement 4)
- [ ] Tap the tag "Heavy cream". Its card opens under the tags, across the full width of the bin. (Story 1, AC 3)
- [ ] Change the sequence to "Low first". No bin head has an amber "low" sticker. "Out" is the first bin. (Story 1, AC 4)
- [ ] Change the sequence to "Use first" and to "A to Z". Each group is a card, and the bins move up into place one after the other. (Story 1, requirements 1 and 6)
- [ ] In "Fridge", the amounts of Spinach, Carrots, and Eggs end at the same right edge. (Story 1, AC 5)
- [ ] Move the pointer over a line. It gets a soft tint. Press it, and it becomes a little smaller. (Story 1, requirement 5)
- [ ] Open the line of milk. The card is on the paper colour, inside the bin. (Story 1, requirement 5 b)
- [ ] On the phone, open Pantry. The bins are in one column. (Story 1, AC 6)

## B. The top stays in view

- [ ] Open Pantry, and scroll to the end of "Kitchen pantry". The nav, the title "Pantry", and the find field stay at the top. The title is smaller, and a thin rule is under the bar. No line shows between the nav and the title. (Story 2, AC 1)
- [ ] Scroll back to the top. The title is large again. (Story 2, AC 5)
- [ ] Open Recipes, and scroll down. The nav, the title "Recipes", and the find field stay at the top. (Story 2, AC 2)
- [ ] Open Plan, and scroll down. The nav, the title "Plan", and the bar stay at the top. (Story 2, AC 3)
- [ ] Open Shop, and scroll down. The nav, the title "Shop", and the strip of the stages stay at the top. (Story 2, AC 4)
- [ ] Do the scroll of the Pantry on the phone. The top stays, and the bar is still one tap to open. (Story 2, requirement 2)
