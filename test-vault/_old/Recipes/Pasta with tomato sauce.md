# Pasta with tomato sauce

A meal for a day with no time. The recipe says only "pasta", so each kind of pasta is good.

*dinner, vegetarian, fast*

**2 servings**

---

- *300 g* pasta
- *1 jar* tomato sauce
- *1* onion
- *2 cloves* garlic
- *50 g* cheese
- salt

---

1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add salt.
2. Cook the pasta for 10 minutes.
3. Cut the onion and the garlic. Cook them in a pan for 5 minutes.
4. Add the tomato sauce. Cook with low heat for 5 minutes.
5. Mix the pasta with the sauce. Put the cheese on top.
