# Check list: open the last part

This list tests the story [Open the last part](../docs/features/v0.6.0/kitchen-open-the-last-part.md), on the phone and on the desktop. Each line has one action and what you must see. The text in brackets is the criterion.

## Before you start

- [ ] The build made `main.js` again. Put the plugin off and on, or restart Obsidian.
- [ ] On the first open after the new build, the kitchen shows Pantry. (Requirement 4)

## A. The tab closes

- [ ] Open Plan. Close the tab of the kitchen. Open the kitchen again. It shows Plan. (AC 1)
- [ ] Open Recipes, and turn on a filter in its bar. Close the tab, and open the kitchen again. It shows Recipes, and the bar has no filter. (AC 4)

## B. Obsidian restarts

- [ ] Open Shop. Close Obsidian fully, and open it again. Open the kitchen. It shows Shop. (AC 2)

## C. Each device

- [ ] On the phone, open Recipes. On the desktop, open Pantry. Wait for Sync. Close and open the kitchen on each device. The phone shows Recipes, and the desktop shows Pantry. (AC 3)
