# Check list: one ledger

This list tests the Pantry story of version 0.9.0, [One ledger](../docs/features/v0.9.0/pantry-one-ledger.md), on the desktop and on the phone. Each line has one action and what you must see. The text in brackets is the criterion or the requirement.

## Before you start

- [ ] The build made `main.js` again. Put the plugin off and on, or restart Obsidian.
- [ ] The Pantry part is on "By place", with no filter and no text in "Find food".

## A. The bins on the desktop

Go to: Pantry, on the desktop.

- [ ] "Freezer" and "4" are over a heavy rule across the full width. No card is around the bin. (AC 1)
- [ ] The stickers "past", "soon", and "low" follow the count in the head of each bin. (Requirement 1 a)
- [ ] Make the tab 1200 px wide. The lines of "Kitchen pantry" are in 4 columns. (AC 2)
- [ ] The columns of "Fridge" line up with the columns of "Kitchen pantry". (AC 2 a)
- [ ] No column is empty while a bin has lines left. The next bin starts under the last row of the bin before it. (AC 2 b, requirement 2)
- [ ] Make the tab as wide as the screen. The bins get more columns, and they still line up. (Requirement 3 a)
- [ ] The bin "Out" has a dashed rule, and its tags wrap across the width. (AC 5)

## B. A card on the desktop

- [ ] Tap the line of Milk. Its card takes the full width of the bin "Fridge", on the surface colour. The other bins step back. (AC 4)
- [ ] Tap "Done" with no change. The card closes, and the lines glide back to their columns. (Requirement 5)
- [ ] Tap the tag of Heavy cream in "Out". Its card takes the full width of "Out". Close it. (Requirement 4)

## C. The phone

Go to: Pantry, on the phone.

- [ ] Each bin has one column of lines, under its heavy rule. (AC 3)
- [ ] Tap the line of Milk. Its card opens in place and moves to the top of the screen. Close it. (Requirement 5)
- [ ] Tap "Low". Only the low lines show, in their bins. Tap "Low" again. (Requirement 5)
