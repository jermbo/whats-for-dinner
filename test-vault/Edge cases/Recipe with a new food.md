# Quinoa bowl

This note tests a recipe with food that the pantry does not know. The shopping list links the quinoa to a note of the pantry folder that is not there, and put away makes that note.

*lunch, vegan*

**2 servings**

---

- *200 g* quinoa
- *400 ml* water
- *1/4 teaspoon* salt

---

1. Wash the quinoa until the water is clear.
2. Put the quinoa, the water, and the salt in a pot. Bring to a boil.
3. Put the lid on. Cook with low heat for 15 minutes.
