# Tomato soup

This note tests properties above the title. Obsidian shows them as properties. The RecipeMD specification does not permit them, because the title must be the first block.

*soup, vegan*

**2 servings**

---

- *1 can* tomatoes
- *1* onion
- *1 tablespoon* oil
- *250 ml* water
- salt

---

1. Cut the onion. Cook it in the oil for 5 minutes.
2. Add the tomatoes and the water. Cook for 15 minutes.
3. Blend the soup. Add salt.
