# Flatbread

This note tests each form of a number in an amount: a mixed fraction, a fraction, a fraction as one character, a decimal with a point, and a decimal with a comma. The yield has a decimal with a comma in a list.

*bread, vegan*

**6 flatbreads, 0,5 kg**

---

- *2 1/2 cups* flour
- *3/4 cup* water
- *½ teaspoon* salt
- *1.5 tablespoons* oil
- *0,5 teaspoon* sugar

---

1. Mix all the ingredients. Knead for 5 minutes.
2. Let the dough rest for 30 minutes.
3. Make 6 flat circles. Cook each in a hot pan for 2 minutes on each side.
